Welcome to Somala

Walk the streets, ride the auto-rickshaw, and watch the village go from sunrise to lamp-lit night.

  • W / S or ▲ ▼ to move, A / D or ◀ ▶ to turn
  • Press E near any vehicle to drive: auto, sports car, tractor or the two bullock carts
  • Walk up to villagers and animals to hear them talk
  • Use the time slider or Dawn · Noon · Dusk · Night
  • Animals and engines have sound, and a song plays whenever you drive. Use ▶ Play song any time
